### Added

- **Production Arabic scan OCR** — official Paddle PP-OCRv5 Arabic ONNX pack, main-process runner, and IPC `ara` route. Arabic-heavy PDFs escalate to this adapter when the pack is present. **Never Tesseract `ara` as the primary path.** Character-reversed ToUnicode PDFs still prefer **DOCX**.
- **Arabic-aware citation mapping** — Arabic bibliography headings, Arabic-Indic numeric cites, and Arabic author-year (`الكتاب، 2020` / narrative) map onto bibliography keys.

### Changed

- Arabic-heavy DOCX deferral lifts when the Paddle pack is registered.
- **Arabic L3 grounding stays unvalidated** until 2.2.0 Sanad Arabic (FT-7). Do not treat Sanad verdicts on Arabic manuscripts as a 2.1.0 claim.
